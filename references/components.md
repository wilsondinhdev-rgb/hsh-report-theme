## J. Global settings (note for reuse)

- Slide 16:9, 960×540 pt; content margins L/R 20–24, top of content y≈115, keep ≥20 bottom.
- Font Arial only (Regular, Bold; Italic for notes). No other families.
- Text color default `#002060`; never pure black.
- Body paragraphs **justified** in cards; captions centered; numbers left.
- Corner radii: cards 12–16, photos 8–12 (some photos square), badges 6, pills 4–14, region cards 24.
- Shadow: soft drop `0 2pt 6pt rgba(0,0,0,.12)`; agenda/icon circles stronger `0 2pt 4pt rgba(0,0,0,.25)`.
- Line weights: hairline dividers 0.5–0.75 pt; accents 1.5–2.4 pt; color rules on cards 3–4 pt.
- Icons: simple line icons (stroke ~1.5 pt) in red/white inside pink circles or solid colored squares; agenda uses white glyphs.
- Photos: bright, realistic interiors/projects/people, often with a red badge overlapping; no filters.
- Density: 3–5 items per row, short UPPERCASE headings + 1–3 justified lines each.
